What Clawdia does

Turn community noise into a weekly plan

Four agents do the reading and thinking. A fifth delivers the result where your team already chats.

Agent 1

Competitor Monitor

Follows what the six competitors announce, change and get praised or blamed for.

Agent 2

Sentiment Analyst

Reads community threads and tracks how players feel about BlueStacks and its rivals.

Agent 3

Trend Spotter

Picks up topics rising across gaming and AI subreddits before they peak.

Agent 4

Content Strategist

Turns the findings into a 7-day content calendar, written with nine bundled reference skills.

How it works

From scattered threads to one Telegram message

1

Collect

Clawdia browses Reddit and gaming and AI subreddits with Apify's RAG Browser and gathers the week's posts about each competitor.

2

Analyze

The Competitor Monitor, Sentiment Analyst and Trend Spotter hand their findings to the Content Strategist, which runs on Claude Sonnet.

3

Deliver

The Delivery Agent sends the report and the 7-day content calendar to Telegram every Monday at 07:00 IST.

Under the hood

One skill, five parallel agents

Clawdia ships as an OpenClaw skill. The agents run in parallel and their findings come together in one weekly report.

Run Clawdia yourself

Install OpenClaw, add the skill, fill in your .env with API tokens, check Telegram with node scripts/test-telegram.js, then schedule it with PM2 cron.
